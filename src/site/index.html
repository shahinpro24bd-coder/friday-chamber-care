About Dr. Md. Raihanul Islam

Newborn & Child Specialist (Pediatrician)

Dr. Md. Raihanul Islam is a newborn and child specialist (pediatrician) serving as Assistant Professor in the Pediatrics Department and In-Charge of the NICU at TMSS Medical College & Rafatullah Community Hospital, Bogura.

He completed his MBBS from Chittagong Medical College and DCH (Child Medicine) from Dhaka Medical College, along with CCD from BIRDEM. He treats newborns and children with careful diagnosis, evidence-based medicine and close follow-up.

Care Philosophy: Accurate diagnosis, safe treatment and clear guidance for parents. Every child receives gentle, attentive care — from newborn intensive care to everyday childhood illness.
