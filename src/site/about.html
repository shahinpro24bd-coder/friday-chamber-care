About Me

Dr. Md. Raihanul Islam

MBBS (Chittagong Medical College) | DCH (Child Medicine, Dhaka Medical College) | CCD (BIRDEM)

Newborn & Child Specialist

Dr. Md. Raihanul Islam is a newborn and child specialist (pediatrician). He serves as Assistant Professor in the Pediatrics Department and as In-Charge of the NICU at TMSS Medical College & Rafatullah Community Hospital, Bogura.

He completed MBBS from Chittagong Medical College and DCH (Child Medicine) from Dhaka Medical College, along with CCD from BIRDEM. His work covers newborn intensive care as well as the everyday illnesses of childhood.

He sees patients at his chamber, Independent General Hospital, Kanchgari, Bogura, from Saturday to Wednesday, 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM. For serial booking: 01713-659355, 01951-393221.
